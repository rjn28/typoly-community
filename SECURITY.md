# Security policy

Typoly handles the text you write, so I give security reports priority.

## Report a vulnerability

Please report it privately, never in a public issue or discussion:

- **On GitHub:** [open a private report](../../security/advisories/new) (Security tab → Report a vulnerability).
- **By email:** [contact@typoly.app](mailto:contact@typoly.app), with "Security" in the subject.

Include what you found, the steps to reproduce it, the Typoly version and what an attacker could gain. I read every report and answer as fast as I can.

## In scope

- The Typoly Mac app
- typoly.app and its account, payment and download pages
- The Typoly correction service the app talks to

## Please do not

- Access, change or delete data that is not yours, or try to read other people's text.
- Run load tests or anything that could slow the service down for others.
- Use social engineering, spam or physical attacks.

I maintain Typoly on my own. I don’t offer a paid bug bounty, and I appreciate the time you take to report a vulnerability.
