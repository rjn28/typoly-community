# Changelog

Every public version of Typoly, newest first. Notes are the ones shown in the app's update window.
Typoly updates itself: you do not need to download anything from this page.

## 1.9.3

<sub>November 10, 2026</sub>

**More reliable in ChatGPT, Slack and Discord.**

Typoly now corrects exactly what you selected in these apps and similar ones, and your clipboard stays as it was. The first correction after opening them is faster too.

Thanks for your reports, keep them coming!

## 1.9.2

<sub>November 2, 2026</sub>

**UPDATE FROM YESTERDAY: every language is corrected.**

Typoly has always detected and corrected every language automatically. Settings now make it clearer: Smart detection comes first, and "Force a language" is only for UK or US spelling, or Hindi / Hinglish and Russian.

**Also new:**

- Pay in dollars or euros: pick $ or € next to the checkout button.

- Clipboard managers like Maccy no longer keep Typoly's corrections.

Thanks for your feedback, keep it coming!

## 1.9.1

<sub>November 2, 2026</sub>

**Choose your correction language.**

You asked for it: pick English (UK), English (US), Hindi / Hinglish or Russian in Settings, or keep automatic detection. Hindi / Hinglish works whether you type in Devanagari or in Latin letters, and never translates your text. Russian corrections are now more accurate.

Thanks to everyone who shared feedback. Every piece of feedback is welcome, so keep it coming! If corrections in your language aren't perfect yet, tell me, with an example if you can.

## 1.9.0

<sub>October 28, 2026</sub>

**Two correction modes. One key.**

Hold for your main correction mode and double-tap for the other. Enable this optional feature in Settings, with a clearer onboarding to help you get started.

A big thank you to everyone who tested it and shared feedback. You helped make this feature more reliable for everyone!

**Also included:** Small improvements.

## 1.8.1

<sub>October 22, 2026</sub>

**A small fix for when your Mac takes a break.**

I’ve fixed an issue that could sign you out of Typoly after sleep. Thanks for reporting it and helping me make Typoly better!

## 1.8.0

<sub>October 13, 2026</sub>

**Introducing EXPERIMENTAL FEATURES**

Your usual Typoly experience stays the same unless you choose to enable an experimental feature.

This update introduces our first experiment: **access both correction modes using the same key.** More experimental features will follow.

To try it, open **Settings → About**, check **“Show experimental features”**, then enable **“Hold and double-tap correction”**.
