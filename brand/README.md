## The name

- **Typoly**, one word, capital T. Not "TypoLy", "Typo-ly" or "TYPOLY".
- In one sentence: *Typoly corrects spelling, grammar and typos in any Mac app, with one key.*

## Please

- Keep the mark as it is: no new colors, no stretching, no effects.
- Please ask for my permission before saying that Typoly sponsors or endorses your work.
